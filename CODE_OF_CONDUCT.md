# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, constructive, and professional. Discuss ideas rather than people, accept good-faith corrections, and respect differing viewpoints.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, trolling, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through the contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially when possible.
